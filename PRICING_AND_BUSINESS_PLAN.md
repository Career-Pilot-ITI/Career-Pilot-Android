## 1. Executive Summary

**Career Pilot** operates on a **Permission-Gate & Coin-Fuel Model**:

1. **Subscription Tiers = Feature Access Permissions:**  
   Subscribing to a tier unlocks the permission to access that specific toolset (e.g., Free unlocks Voice only; Plus unlocks Voice + ATS; Max unlocks Voice + ATS + Video + Quizzes).
2. **Coins = Universal Consumption Fuel:**  
   **Every single usage action always consumes coins** across all tiers. There are no artificial "free monthly session quotas" (e.g. no "1 session/month in Free or 2 in Plus"). An interview session *always* costs 20 Coins, an ATS scan *always* costs 5 Coins, and so on.
3. **High Net Profitability:**  
   Because every action is metered through coins, the platform eliminates infrastructure abuse while maintaining **> 98% gross margins** on AI operations (~0.18 EGP delivery cost per voice interview).

## 6. Commercial Rationale: Why This Model Wins

1. **No Artificial "Monthly Quota" Confusion:**  
   By eliminating rules like *"Free has 1 interview and Plus has 2"*, the user experience is clean and intuitive: **The plan decides what tools you can open, and coins fuel your usage**.
2. **Guaranteed Revenue Per Action:**  
   Because every single turn burns coins, there is zero risk of bot abuse or unprofitable high-frequency users draining server resources.
3. **High Perceived Value & Low Entry Barrier:**  
   A candidate can enter on **FREE** and purchase a **149 EGP coin pack** to run 2 voice interviews. When they need to optimize their CV for an ATS, they upgrade to **PLUS (349 EGP)** to unlock the ATS gates.
4. **Immediate CAC Payback:**  
   At **349 EGP (PLUS)** and **599 EGP (MAX)**, subscription fees immediately cover marketing acquisition costs (~150–180 EGP) on day one.
