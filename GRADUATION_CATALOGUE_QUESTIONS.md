# Career Pilot — Graduation Catalogue & Project Submission Record

---

## Project name
Career Pilot

---

## Tagline
AI-powered career guidance, ATS job optimization, and voice-enabled mock interview platform with interactive competitive challenges.

---

## The problem
Job seekers and recent graduates face high rejection rates at both the resume screening and interview stages because over 75% of resumes are automatically filtered out by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) for unaligned keywords, while private 1-on-1 interview coaching is prohibitively expensive and inaccessible. Candidates submit generic resumes, lack job-specific cover letters, and practice interviews in isolation without real-time evaluation or peer benchmarking. Generic AI chatbots fail to fix this — they cannot parse live job posting URLs, lack structured ATS match scoring, suffer from speech recognition latency during voice practice, and provide no competitive challenge environment for recruiters or communities to test talent under real interview conditions.

---

## Your solution
Career Pilot runs a unified Career AI Orchestration Engine featuring an ATS Job Workspace and an Interview Challenge Platform. Candidates import job listings via URL or text share to extract standardized requirements, generating ATS resume match scores, keyword gap analyses, AI-tailored resume rewrites, and custom cover letters. For interview preparation, the engine pairs on-device voice transcription with multi-criteria AI rubrics to score answers on technical accuracy, clarity, and pacing. Additionally, a competitive Challenge Module enables creators and recruiters to host public or private interview competitions with live leaderboards, entry coin stakes, and performance analytics dashboards.

---

## Project overview
Career Pilot is a comprehensive career preparation and interview platform that helps job seekers optimize their job applications, practice for interviews, and benchmark their skills. Users import job postings to create dedicated ATS workspaces that analyze resume compatibility, rewrite CVs, generate cover letters, and launch job-tailored voice mock interviews. Candidates and creators can also host or join competitive interview challenges with global leaderboards, progress tracking, and detailed scorecard reports. By unifying ATS resume optimization, voice interview simulation, performance analytics, and competitive challenge boards into a single portal, Career Pilot provides job seekers with a 24/7 personal career coach.

---

## Key features
ATS Job Workspace — Creates dedicated job containers by importing job URLs, raw descriptions, or shared listings from external applications.
ATS Match Score & Keyword Gap Analysis — Evaluates resumes against specific job postings to report overall match percentages, missing keywords, strengths, and weaknesses.
AI Resume Optimization & Rewrite — Automatically rewrites candidate resumes to match target job descriptions and maximize ATS screening pass rates.
Tailored Cover Letter Generator — Produces customized, job-aligned cover letters matching candidate experience to extracted job requirements.
Job-Specific Voice Mock Interviews — Launches multi-turn voice practice sessions auto-configured around imported job posting requirements rather than generic topics.
Public & Private Interview Challenges — Enables creators, recruiters, and communities to host competitive interview contests with custom time limits, coin entry fees, and private invite links.
Live Challenge Leaderboards — Ranks challenge participants in real time based on AI interview scores, response accuracy, and completion speeds.
Recruiter & Creator Analytics Dashboard — Gives challenge hosts detailed candidate overview metrics, individual AI reports, question breakdowns, and coaching insights.
On-Device Voice Transcription — Transcribes spoken interview answers locally using speech recognition models to eliminate audio lag and cloud processing latency.
Deterministic Multi-Criteria Answer Scoring — Grades interview answers against objective technical accuracy, speech pacing, and domain vocabulary rubrics.
Diagnostic Performance Scorecards — Generates detailed session reports with radar charts, skill breakdowns, and actionable feedback to highlight improvement areas.
Tiered Subscriptions & Coin Wallet — Provides Free, Plus, and Max plans alongside a coin top-up wallet for entry fees and pay-as-you-go session access.
